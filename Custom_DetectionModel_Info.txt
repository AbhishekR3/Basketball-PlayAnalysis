The dataset includes 2870 images.
Classes annotated in YOLOv8 format
- Basketball
- Team_A
- Team_B

The following pre-processing was applied to each image:
* Auto-orientation of pixel data (with EXIF-orientation stripping)
* Resize to 640x640 (Stretch)

The following augmentation was applied to create 3 versions of each source image:
* 50% probability of horizontal flip
* 50% probability of vertical flip
* Equal probability of one of the following 90-degree rotations: none, clockwise, counter-clockwise
* Random rotation of between -10 and +10 degrees
* Salt and pepper noise was applied to 0.49 percent of pixels


********************************************************************************************************************

Overall, the model performs significantly better than the generic pre-trained YOLO model.
The custom model records the following metrics:
- mAP50     - 99.5%
- mAP50-95  - 90.7%
- Precision - 99.6%
- Recall    - 99.6%
